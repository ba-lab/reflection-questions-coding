# Assignment for students to reflect on their coding process

This assignment invites you to look back at how you worked on your coding project and reflect on your coding habits. Use the online version of your coding process report (not the PDF) so you can click on and explore the charts. When answering, include dates, times, and examples from your report.

**A. Reflect on your overall coding process**

1. How did you approach the task? Describe your first plan after reading the instructions and how it changed as you worked, for example whether you tried alternative solutions, sought help, or searched online.
2. Recall a moment when you felt stuck or frustrated. What did you do to move forward, and what did that moment reveal about gaps in your knowledge or your habits as a problem solver?
3. Looking at your timeline, was your time management effective? Consider how long you worked, when you took breaks, and whether those breaks helped you return with new ideas or broke your momentum.

**B. Reflect on your debugging and testing habits**  

4. How many times did you run your code, and how many runs ended in errors? Find your longest streak of failed runs in the report and describe what you were trying to fix.
5. What was your most common debugging strategy (e.g., trial and error, print statements, reading error messages)? Was it effective, and is there evidence that you switched strategies when one wasn't working?
6. Which types of errors did you run into most often? Do they point to a recurring misunderstanding, such as a repeated syntax issue or the same logic mistake?
7. When and how did you check that your code was correct: did you plan test cases before coding, test as you went, or test only at the end? What might your approach have missed?

**C. Reflect on your AI use** 

8. Which AI tools did you use, and for what purposes (e.g., planning, generating code, debugging, explaining concepts)? How did your use of AI compare with looking things up yourself?
9. Describe a time when an AI tool gave you incorrect, confusing, or unhelpful output. How did you notice, and how did you check or correct it?

**D. Look ahead** 

10. If you were to do this task again, what would you change? Name one specific habit you would keep and one you would change, based on what your report showed.
